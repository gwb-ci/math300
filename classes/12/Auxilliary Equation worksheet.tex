{\bf Activity 1}:  In small group, work to find the initial conditions and a recurrence relation for one (or more) of the following problems.

\begin{packed_enum}
\item $a_n$ is the number of ways to tile a $3 \times n$ board with $1 \times 1$ and $ 2\times 2$ tiles. 
\item $b_n$ is the number of length-$n$ ternary strings where no two consecutive letters are nonzero. (That is, zeros can be adjacent, non-zeros cannot be adjacent.  For example a substrings of 11, 12, 21, or 22 are not allowed.)
\item $c_n$ is the number of ways to tile a $2 \times n$ board with $1 \times 2$ and $ 2\times 2$ tiles.
\item $d_n$ is the number of ways to tile a $1 \times n$ board with $1 \times 1$  white tiles, $ 1\times 2$ grey tiles and $ 1\times 2$ black tiles.
\end{packed_enum}

{\bf Activity 2}:  In small groups, work on the following problems sequentially.
\begin{packed_enum}

\item Find the closed solution to the recurrence relation using the auxiliary equation method $a_n=3a_{n-1}+4a_{n-2}$
with initial terms $a_0=2$ and $a_1=3$.
\item The recursive formula for Fibonacci's sequence is $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ with $F_0=F_1=1$.  Find the auxiliary equation, roots, and general solution to this recurrence.
\item The recursive formula for Narayana's cow sequence is $C_n=C_{n-1}+C_{n-2}+C_{n-3}$ with $C_0=C_1=C_2=1$.  Find the auxiliary equation for this recurrence.

\end{packed_enum}
