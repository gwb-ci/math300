{\bf Problem 1}: For each of the below problems, first answer the following two questions:  ``Does the order of the choices matter? (Order:yes or no)''  and ``Is repetition of choices allowed?'' (Repetition: yes or no)\\

Suppose you have a {\bf huge} box of animal crackers containing plenty of each of 10 different animals. 

\begin{enumerate}
\item  How many animal parades containing 6 crackers can you line up? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item How many animal parades of 6 crackers can you line up so that the animals appear in alphabetical order? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item  How many ways could you line up 6 different animals in alphabetical order? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item  How many ways could you line up 6 different animals if they can come in any order? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item  How many ways could you give 6 children one animal cracker each? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item  How many ways could you give 6 children one animal cracker each so that no two kids get the same animal? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\item  How many ways could you give out 6 giraffes to 10 kids? Order:yes or no \& Repetition: yes or no\\
\end{enumerate}
