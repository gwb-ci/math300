      patterns..."  --  Lynn A. Steen, mathematician
\vfill

\vfill
``Mathematics is the science of patterns, and nature exploits just about every pattern that there is.'' - Ian Stewart, mathematician
\vfill
``Doing mathematics should always mean finding patterns and crafting beautiful and meaningful explanations'' - Paul Lockhart, teacher
\vfill
``A mathematician, like a painter or poet, is a maker of patterns. If his patterns are more permanent than theirs, it is because they are made with ideas.'' - G. H. Hardy, mathematician

\frame
{
  \frametitle{Advice from past students}

Former student's answer to the question What advice would you give students to be successful in this class?" at the end of the semester:\vfill

\begin{packed_enum}
\item Start homework early, do it consistently, and redo missed or difficult problems since homework closely matches exam questions.
\item Attend every lecture, pay attention, take clear notes, and ask questions to fully understand concepts and proofs.
\item Use support resources regularly, including the Learning Resource Center, office hours, review sessions, and practice exams.

\end{packed_enum}
\vfill
}


\frame
{
  \frametitle{More advice from past students}

Former student's answer to the question What advice would you give students to be successful in this class?" at the end of the semester: \vfill

\begin{packed_enum}

\item Form study groups early, collaborate with classmates, and communicate through group chats or Discord.
\item Practice star problems, proofs, and pretests, and build a helpful formula sheet with key concepts and examples.
\item Take advantage of extra credit opportunities and manage your time, stress, and sleep to stay on track.
\end{packed_enum}
}

  \frametitle{Tiling with Triominos}
In small groups, work on the ``Tiling with Triominos'' worksheet. The goals of this worksheet are to:

\begin{packed_enum}
\item Experience the challenge of understanding what a question is asking
\item Practice the problem solving strategy of drawing pictures.
\item Practice the problem solving strategy of working on ''Small n'' versions of a general problem.
\end{packed_enum}
